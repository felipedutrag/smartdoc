Tiptap wraps the proven ProseMirror library in a modern, framework-agnostic API and gives you extensions and features for everything. What is Tiptap? Tiptap is a headless rich-text editor framework that lets you build a custom editor completely tailored to your and your customers&#x27; needs. It&#x27;s built on top of ProseMirror, a battle-tested library for building rich-text editors on the web. Under the hood Tiptap heavily relies on Events , Commands , and Extensions to provide a flexible and powerful API for building editors. Why pick Tiptap? Modular by default: Add only the extensions you need, from bold and links to complex tables and slash-menus. Keep the bundle small and your schema under control.
